# .archive — the pre-vault direction of draft 7.0

> **Archived 2026-08-10, wholesale.** Everything in this folder was the draft's position between **2026-08-05**
> (the turn away from draft 6.0) and **2026-08-10** (the vault). It is kept because it is the record of how the
> draft got here — **not** because any of it is still active.

---

## Why it was archived

The author's own reason, verbatim:

> ของพวกนั้นเป็นเเค่ direction ที่ตั้งมาไว้ให้ debate เพื่อค้นคว้าข้อมูล เเบบไม่มีอะไร ref เลยเฉยๆ เเล้วส่วนใหญ่โดนตีตกไปหมดเเล้วด้วย

That is exactly what these documents were built to be, and they did their job. The draft's own rule was
*"hold a standing candidate, and attack it with rivals — not with opinions"*, and the position was explicitly
labelled **the first decision, not the final one**, held at **evidence zero** so that research would have a target.

Three days of study ([`../notes/vault/`](../notes/vault/), 15 files, ~770 KB) produced the rivals. The old picture
was **replaced wholesale and dated**, which is what the draft said it would do when that happened.

**The live position is now [`../docs/direction.md`](../docs/direction.md).**

## Rules for this folder

1. **Do not edit anything in here.** It is a record of a position that was held and then replaced. Editing it
   destroys the only evidence of how the draft moved.
2. **Do not cite it as current.** Anything quoted from here must be marked as the pre-2026-08-10 position.
3. **Restoring is allowed, but only whole and only deliberately** — the same rule the draft applied to replacing a
   standing candidate. If a piece of this comes back, it comes back into
   [`../docs/direction.md`](../docs/direction.md) with a date and a reason, not by quietly un-archiving a file.
